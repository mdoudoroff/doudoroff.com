Monday Mood

Started with the bass line: AJH stuff. Developed three additional parts that are modulated E370 wavetables: one drone and the two other melodic patterns in higher registers. These were all sequenced and partially modulated by the OXI ONE. Nothing exotic going on here; filters are in the audio paths. Except for the drone, these are sent (to some extent) to the Samarkanda patched as a ping-pong delay. The drone gets some processing in Swells to kind of fluff it out with one of their weirder, unnatural algorithms.

All this I recorded together, wiggling some cutoffs by hand, and keeping the third take. The I went back and constructed the percussion parts and multitracked those in. The clattery/crashing sounds are actually the BIA processed through Swells, abusing the bucket brigade algorithm. I liked how that worked out. The other two drums are just euclidean sequences triggering Assimil8or samples. I used the performance features of the OXI to manipulate the pitterpatter so it didn’t just do the same thing endlessly.

Not a lot of editing, just some levels and bringing things in/out to give the sketch some form. I think I threw some EQ on a couple parts to get them to not stomp each other as much.
